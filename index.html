FEATURES

Everything you need for a creative sandbox, running at full speed in a single browser tab.

🌍Procedural worlds

Rolling hills, sandy beaches, oceans, lakes and forests, generated in a few seconds.

⛏️Mine and build

Hold to mine, right-click to place. Pick any block with middle click and pull it into your hotbar.

🕳️Caves and ores

Winding tunnels run under the hills, with coal and iron veins hiding in the stone and diamonds buried deep down.

🌊Real water

Dive in and swim. The world goes blue and foggy underwater.

🧟Night mobs

When the sun sets, zombies and creepers come out. Fight them off or build a shelter.

🎒Survival and creative

Mine blocks, craft a table and pickaxes from wood to diamond, and sleep in a bed to skip the night. Press G for creative mode with flying.

BLOCKS

Seventeen blocks and items, from humble dirt to crafting tables, beds and diamonds.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Terracube is free and runs entirely in your browser.

Will my world be saved?

Not yet. Every visit starts from the same generated world, and your changes are lost when you close the tab.

Can I play on my phone?

Not well. The game needs a keyboard and mouse, so use a desktop or laptop.

Is there multiplayer or survival mode?

There is a survival mode with health, mining times, item drops, a crafting table, wood-to-diamond pickaxes, beds and night-time zombies and creepers. Press G in game for creative mode. There is no multiplayer yet.

The game is slow. What can I do?

Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration turned on.
